# Prodwise — submission text

## Project description

Prodwise is a product-management workspace that turns scattered product evidence into a traceable initiative record, highlights decisions that need human attention, derives a Roadmap from confirmed delivery facts, and uses Claude to draft a shared weekly management review.

The MVP includes Home, an initiative register, Brief, Decisions, Knowledge and Sources, delivery history, Roadmap, Weekly Review and Analysis. A separate synthetic Demo organization gives the course reviewer full organization-level access without exposing real business data.

## Problem statement

Product managers reconstruct initiative status from requirements, meeting notes, implementation records and changing delivery dates. Conflicting values and outdated requirements can survive across those sources. Weekly management updates then take manual effort and may hide missing information or lose the reason a target changed.

## AI solution statement

Claude drafts weekly review wording from the initiative facts, recorded decisions and changes since the previous finalized review. The application validates accepted AI statements against supported references, preserves unknowns and retains a deterministic template fallback. Humans review and save the initiative sections before finalizing an immutable weekly snapshot. AI does not approve a release or silently update delivery truth.

## What the MVP demonstrates

- A structured, source-linked record and explicit human decisions.
- A deterministic 27-versus-30 calculation mismatch, with a separate explicitly superseded financing requirement.
- A Roadmap derived from human-confirmed facts, including a target movement from 1 to 8 October and preserved revision history.
- One shared weekly review with a previous finalized baseline, a real Claude drafting step and required human review.
- Honest Analysis: portfolio statistics from existing records; business outcome measurement left unconfigured when no approved observations exist.

## Intended user

Product Managers / Product Owners, Product Leads and executives reviewing a portfolio of initiatives.

## Data disclosure

The reviewer organization contains synthetic initiatives, evidence, delivery dates and reviews. Its data is curated for demonstration and is not an AMAN dataset. Portfolio counts describe those synthetic records. No transaction, revenue, conversion or commercial outcome is claimed.
