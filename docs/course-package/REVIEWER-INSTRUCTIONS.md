# Prodwise — reviewer instructions

Welcome to Prodwise. Your pre-provisioned account opens **Prodwise Demo**, a separate organization containing synthetic product scenarios.

Use the MVP link and credentials supplied privately with the submission. Sign in directly; no signup, invite acceptance or organization configuration is required. Your account has **ORG_OWNER** permissions in this Demo organization only. It has no platform role and no access to AMAN or another real organization.

## Suggested walkthrough

1. **Home:** inspect the portfolio attention and current delivery changes.
2. **Initiatives → Merchant Flex Finance → Brief:** orient yourself in the fictional merchant-finance pilot.
3. **Knowledge / Sources:** inspect the recorded requirements and supporting source summaries. The Islamic-only financing decision explicitly replaces the earlier financing scope.
4. **Decisions:** compare the daily repayment divisors **27** and **30** together with their source context. You may exercise the decision workflow.
5. **Delivery facts:** inspect the pilot scope, confirmation details and Target Live history **1 October → 8 October**.
6. **Roadmap:** see that confirmed initiative facts produce the portfolio timing. Unknown dates remain explicit.
7. **Weekly Review:** open **2026-W39**, with finalized **2026-W38** as its initial comparison baseline. Refresh the draft if your earlier edits changed its inputs.
8. Generate a **Claude draft**. Read the AI/fallback label and supported wording. Review and save all initiative sections before finalizing.
9. **Analysis:** inspect real counts of these synthetic records and the honest project-metric configuration checklist.
10. Open **Guide** whenever you need help; it is dismissible and saves your place in this browser.

## What to expect

- You can create, edit and make decisions inside the Demo organization.
- AI drafts wording. People confirm delivery facts and finalize the review.
- A missing Actual Live date does not prove that a launch failed.
- Finalizing a weekly review freezes its comparison snapshot; it does not grant business or release approval.
- Claude availability depends on the live provider. If unavailable, the interface labels the deterministic template fallback clearly.
- Business KPI data is intentionally not fabricated. No transaction or commercial performance dataset is connected.
- The canonical scenario uses September–October 2026 dates and a visible **Scenario date · 26 September 2026**. This keeps the synthetic story consistent when reviewed later.
- Seeded dates and the previous finalized review are labelled **Synthetic scenario preparation**. They are prepared demonstration records, not actions you previously performed. Your own edits are attributed to your signed-in account.
- Demo access is limited to the pre-created reviewer. Ordinary invitations to other addresses are refused by the organization policy.

Changes persist inside Demo. If you want the original scenario restored after experimentation, ask the project owner to run the operator-only Demo reset. Credentials and reset controls are not published in this document.
